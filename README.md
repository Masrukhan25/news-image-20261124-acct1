# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

A broadsheet newspaper summing up 24 November 2026 in one image: 31 verified
stories across 6 regions, drawn entirely in flat vector with PIL.

- **3 heroes:** IRGC's Hormuz tanker-mine claim, Russian jets triggering the
  Ulleung air-raid alert, Sanofi's $41 billion Insmed deal.
- **Grid:** stories 4–15 with flat-vector icons and red region banners.
- **Map:** every story plotted at its true lon/lat, with leader lines and a
  per-region count legend.
- **Also on Tuesday:** stories 16–31 in two columns.
- **The Day in Numbers:** $41bn, $290bn, 5.6, 6 killed, 23, 10m tons, 5%.
- **Date Check:** stories rejected for the wrong date, listed honestly.

`build.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG). `main.py` copies it to
`/output/entry.png` (honours `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`). Every story happened or
was first reported on 24 November 2026; research cutoff 08:30 UTC.
